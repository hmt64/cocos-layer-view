Cocos Anatomy is installed

Explode a running Cocos Creator game (2.x and 3.x) into a 3D layer view.

  1. Open a page that runs a Cocos Creator game.
  2. Press Alt + Shift + L, or click the extension icon in the toolbar. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu to keep it in sight.
  3. Press the same shortcut or click the icon again to leave the layer view.

The extension only runs on the tab where you activate it. If the shortcut does not respond, check or change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
